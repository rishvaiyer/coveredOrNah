SPECIALTY AUDIT BEFORE CLAIMING COMPLETE OUTPATIENT SCOPE
- Product-specific inhaler strengths and devices for all ICS, LABA, LAMA, ICS/LABA, and triple therapies.
- PAH infusion products and hospital-administered therapies.
- NTM and resistant-airway infection regimens.
- ILD immunosuppressants and transplant workflows.
- Sleep medicine/PAP-adjacent medicines, only if that service is included.

RULES
- Keep inhaler, nebulizer, oral, injection, infusion, and hospital-administered products distinct.
- Device, strength, route, brand, and generic can have different coverage.
- Add an official payer source before presenting a covered/not-covered result.
- Unsourced plan/product combinations are Unconfirmed, never a denial.
