# Formulary Finder: product brief

## What can be sold now

Formulary Finder is ready to demonstrate and sell as a supervised, PHI-free New Jersey clinic design-partner proof. The buyer is paying for a faster, source-visible medication formulary workflow, not a guarantee of eligibility, payment, approval, or prescribing advice.

### Current product proof

- 85 pulmonary and commonly used medication families in the quick-search catalog.
- 17 NJ plan-family baselines with insurer and coverage-type filtering.
- Exact CMS plan selection for NJ Medicare Advantage and standalone Part D.
- Exact or payer-published product checks for UHC Marketplace, Aetna NJ FamilyCare, UHC Community NJ Medicaid, Fidelis NJ FamilyCare, Horizon NJ Health, and Wellpoint NJ FamilyCare.
- Full Wellpoint NJ FamilyCare feed: 170,121 NDC rows and 34,977 normalized products, effective August 1, 2026.
- Source-visible results with preferred tier, prior authorization, step therapy, quantity limit, specialty, and other published restrictions when supplied.
- “Unconfirmed, not a denial” handling when a complete exact source match is unavailable.

## Recommended offer

### 60-day design-partner proof

- One NJ clinic or specialty group.
- Up to ten prescribers and unlimited support staff.
- PHI-free test scenarios only.
- Clinic supplies its ten to twenty highest-volume plan families and medications.
- Weekly review of false matches, unconfirmed results, and source freshness.
- Success measures: median lookup time, exact-plan selection rate, correction rate, and percentage of scenarios resolved in-portal.

### What the buyer receives

- Hosted portal access.
- Clinician user guide and five-minute demo.
- Source matrix and refresh notes.
- Named proof owner and issue log.
- A prioritized connector roadmap based on the clinic’s real volume.

## Do not promise yet

- Patient-specific eligibility or cost.
- Guaranteed coverage or approval.
- Prior-authorization submission.
- EHR integration or member-ID lookup.
- HIPAA/BAA readiness without a separate security and contracting workstream.

## Fastest path to a paid contract

1. Demo the exact-plan workflow to a NJ clinic leader and one nurse or pharmacist.
2. Run ten de-identified scenarios from the clinic’s highest-volume plans.
3. Measure time saved and mismatches against the clinic’s current verification process.
4. Convert the proof into a paid annual subscription only after the source-refresh owner and security requirements are agreed.
